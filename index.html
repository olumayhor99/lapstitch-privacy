Lapstitch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Lapstitch does not collect, transmit or store any personal information. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you open in this app is sent anywhere.

This policy describes what the app does with the material you give it. It is short because there is little to describe.

Your screenshots

Screenshots are chosen through the system picker, which hands the app only the images you select. Lapstitch never requests access to your photo library, so it cannot see anything you have not picked.

The images are copied into the app's own storage on your device, read, analysed and combined there, and the finished image is written to the app's storage on the same device. All of this happens locally. The app has no networking code of any kind and works fully in Aeroplane Mode.

Copies of the screenshots you selected are deleted when the job that used them finishes. Finished images stay on your device until you delete them, either individually from Recent or all at once from Settings.

Saving to your photo library

Saving a finished image asks for add-only permission. That permission allows the app to add a photo and nothing else — it does not allow it to read, browse or modify anything already in your library. You can decline, and everything except saving continues to work.

Sharing screenshots into Lapstitch

When you share screenshots from another app, the share extension copies them into a shared container on your device (group.com.ademolaolatunji.Lapstitch) and hands them to the main app. Those copies are deleted as soon as the app has taken them, and anything left behind by an interrupted share is swept automatically.

Editing in Photos

Lapstitch's Photos editing extension receives the single image you chose to edit, applies the areas you covered, and returns the result to Photos. Photos keeps your original and the edit can be reverted. The image is not copied anywhere else and does not leave your device.

Buying Lapstitch Pro

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Lapstitch never sees your payment details, your Apple Account, or your name. The app asks the system whether a valid purchase exists and receives only that answer.

Along with the rating prompt described below, buying or restoring Pro is one of only two things in the app that reach the network, and both of those connections are Apple's, not ours.

Ratings

After a save that worked, the app may ask iOS to show Apple's rating prompt. Whether it appears at all is iOS's decision, and anything you rate goes to Apple — the app never sees it, and there is no way for us to tell who rated what.

All the app keeps is a count of how many stitches you have saved and the date it last asked, both stored on your device in the app's own settings and never sent anywhere.

Crash reports and diagnostics

Lapstitch contains no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party SDKs of any kind. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with anonymised crash reports. That sharing is controlled by you in iOS Settings and can be turned off there at any time; the app itself collects nothing and cannot identify you from it.

Children

Lapstitch collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and in the app, with a new date at the top. Because the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a change in what happens to your data.
